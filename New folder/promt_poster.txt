The Master Prompt
A premium, modern vertical poster for an overseas education consultancy named "ApplyNFly". The design features a corporate "startup" aesthetic with a deep navy to royal blue gradient background. Top section: Bold logo text "ApplyNFly" with a small graduation cap integrated, and tagline "Trusted Study Abroad Partner" below it. An airplane icon flies from the left with a smooth, curved blue trail arching over the center. Main Headline: "Launch Your Global Future" in large, bold sans-serif font, followed by subheadline "Study Abroad Made Simple." Left column includes "Study Abroad Guidance" and "Documentation & Visa" with bullet points. Right column includes "Financial Support" and "Student Support" with bullet points. Lower section: A row of circular country flags (USA, UK, Canada, Germany, Australia, France, Singapore, NZ) above the text "Top Study Destinations". Professional 4K quality, clean layout, Montserrat or Poppins typography, minimal line icons, high-end branding style.

Tips for "Exact" Replication:
Text Rendering: While modern models (like Gemini 3 Flash or Midjourney v6) are great at text, they can still make typos. You may need to run the prompt 2–3 times to get every word spelled correctly.

Aspect Ratio: If using Midjourney, add --ar 4:5 to the end of the prompt.

The "Glass" Effect: The key term is "Glassmorphism". If the box isn't transparent enough, emphasize "frosted glass effect with background blur."

Flag Row: If the flags come out messy, you can edit that specific area or request "clean, flat circular flag icons."
