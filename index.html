FAQ

Common questions

Planning time depends on your intended intake, course, and institution. Check application dates and document requirements directly with each institution and allow time to prepare.

Consider your academic background, interests, course content, language of instruction, location, and budget. Compare details from institutions before deciding.

Institutions commonly request academic transcripts and completion certificates. Some courses may ask for additional materials; check the official course application guidance for the exact list.

Requirements depend on the institution, course, and your education history. Check which tests, if any, are accepted by your chosen institutions.

Some programmes request these tests and others do not. Review the current admissions requirements for each course you are considering.

Scholarship availability and eligibility vary by institution and programme. Loan terms are set by lenders; review their current criteria and terms before applying.

Visa processes and timelines can change. Consult the relevant official government guidance for the destination and apply according to its current instructions.
